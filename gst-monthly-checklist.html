A simple monthly checklist for GST returns

A calm routine for GST each month, so returns are accurate and nothing is left to the last day.

Guide · 5 min read·By the DAIR Consultants team

Most GST problems are not caused by the return itself. They come from records that were not checked during the month. A short routine makes the filing itself straightforward.

Before the month ends

  • Issue invoices correctly. Check the customer's GSTIN, the place of supply and the tax rate on every invoice.
  • Collect purchase invoices. Ask suppliers for any bills that are missing, and check that their GSTIN is correct.
  • Record everything in your books. Sales, purchases, credit notes and debit notes should all be entered.

In the first week of the new month

  1. Reconcile your sales register with your books and your bank receipts.
  2. Prepare the outward supplies return (GSTR-1) from the reconciled sales register.
  3. Check that credit notes and amendments are reported in the right period.

Before you pay tax

  1. Compare your purchase register with the input tax credit shown in GSTR-2B.
  2. Follow up with suppliers whose invoices are missing from GSTR-2B.
  3. Claim only the credit you are eligible for, and keep notes on anything held back.
  4. Prepare the summary return (GSTR-3B), pay the tax due and file it.

After filing

  • Save the acknowledgements and payment challans with your records.
  • Note any differences you need to correct next month.
  • Update your compliance calendar for the next set of due dates.

Due dates depend on your turnover and the filing scheme you have chosen, so confirm the dates that apply to your registration on the GST portal.

This guide is general information, not advice for your specific situation. Rules and due dates can change, so please check the latest position or talk to us before acting.
