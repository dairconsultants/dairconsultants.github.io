FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do we need clean data before we start?

No. The readiness audit tells you exactly which data matters, and quick wins often start with the data you already have.

Which AI models and tools do you use?

We're vendor-neutral and pick the right model for each job, from leading AI providers to open-source models that run in your own environment.

Will our data be used to train AI models?

No. We use enterprise agreements with no training on your data, and we can deploy on your own cloud if needed.

Can AI Labs work for teams outside finance?

Yes. We focus on finance and back-office operations, but the same approach works for procurement, HR operations and customer support.
