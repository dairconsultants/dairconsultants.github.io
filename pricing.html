FAQ

Pricing questions

What counts as a transaction?

Any line on your bank, card or payment-gateway statements. We review your last three months during the discovery call to recommend the right plan.

Is there a setup fee?

Onboarding is free on Growth and Scale plans. If your past books need catching up, the clean-up is quoted separately.

Can I change plans?

Yes. You can move up or down at the start of any month. If your volume grows, we'll suggest the right plan, never surprise you with overage fees.

Is there a lock-in?

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are billed upfront at a 15% discount.

Do prices include GST?

No. GST at 18% is added to all fees.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your business and we'll recommend a plan and send a fixed-fee proposal within 48 hours.
