(01) Overview

AI tools can save hours of routine work, but only when they fit how your team actually works. We help you start small, choose sensibly, keep your data safe, and make sure people stay in charge of the results.

(02) What is included

Practical help, step by step.

  • 01

    Process review

    We map your current workflows and find the tasks worth automating.

  • 02

    Tool selection

    Independent advice on which tools fit your needs and budget.

  • 03

    Set-up and integration

    We configure the tools and connect them to your existing systems.

  • 04

    Team training

    Hands-on sessions so your team can use the tools with confidence.

  • 05

    Data safety and controls

    Sensible rules on access, review and data handling.

  • 06

    Ongoing review

    We check what is working and adjust as your needs change.

(04) Who it is for

Finance and operations teams spending too much time on routine work, and owners who want sensible, practical advice on using AI safely.

(05) Questions

Good to know.

Do we need to be technical to work with you?+

No. We explain everything in plain language and handle the technical set-up for you.

Which AI tools do you recommend?+

We are independent and recommend tools based on your needs, budget and data, not on any partnership.

Will our data be safe?+

Data safety is part of every recommendation. We help you set clear rules on what data is used, who can access it and how results are checked.

Can you help teams outside finance?+

Yes. Our focus is finance and back-office operations, but the same approach works for other routine processes.
