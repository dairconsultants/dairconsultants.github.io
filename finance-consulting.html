FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is a virtual CFO different from a full-time CFO?

You get the same senior expertise for the days you actually need it, typically 2 to 6 days a month, at around a quarter of the cost of a full-time hire.

Do I need to use Dair for bookkeeping too?

It's not required, but it helps. Advice built on our live ledger is faster and more accurate. If you have an existing bookkeeper, we'll work with them.

Can you help us raise money?

We build the financial model, prepare the data room and support you through due diligence. We don't act as brokers or take success fees.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a week of the discovery call. The first deliverable, a baseline forecast, follows within two weeks.
