(05) Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to use DAIR for bookkeeping too?+

No, but it helps. If another firm keeps your books, we will work with them and their records.

How often will we meet?+

Most clients meet us monthly. During a fundraise or a busy period we can meet more often.

Can you help us raise money?+

We prepare the financial model, projections and supporting documents, and help you answer investors' financial questions. We do not act as brokers.

How are fees set?+

We agree a fixed monthly fee or a project fee in writing before work starts, based on the scope we agree together.
