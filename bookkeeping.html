(05) Questions

Good to know.

Which accounting software do you use?+

We work in Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Xero. If you have not chosen one yet, we will recommend a good fit and set it up for you.

How do I send you my documents?+

Whatever is easiest for you: email, a shared folder, or direct access to your accounting software. We agree the routine together during onboarding.

Can you sort out past records that are behind?+

Yes. We often start by catching up on previous months or years, then move to a regular monthly routine. We quote for the catch-up separately so you know the cost upfront.

How is my data kept confidential?+

We sign a confidentiality agreement with every client, limit access to the people working on your account, and use secure, access-controlled tools.
