FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which accounting software do you work with?

We work in Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks and Xero. If you don't have one yet, we'll recommend and set up the best fit for you.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and we sign an NDA and data processing agreement with every client. AI models never train on your data.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Every AI-categorised item carries a confidence score. Anything below our threshold goes to your accountant, and every month is reviewed and signed off by a qualified person.

Can you clean up past books?

Yes. Most clients start with a one-time clean-up of the last 6 to 24 months, which we usually finish in 2 to 4 weeks.

How much does it cost?

Plans start from a fixed monthly fee based on transaction volume. See our pricing page for details.
