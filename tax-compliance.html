Tax & compliance

Returns filed carefully
and on time.

We prepare your returns from well-kept records, keep track of every due date, and file before the deadline.

Book a free consultation
(01) Overview

Compliance is easier when the books are right. We prepare each return from reconciled records, check the details carefully, and keep you informed about what is due and when.

(02) What is included

Every return, handled properly.

  • 01

    GST returns

    Regular GST returns, reconciliation of input tax credit and annual returns.

  • 02

    TDS and TCS

    Deductions checked, payments made on time and quarterly returns filed.

  • 03

    Income tax

    Advance tax planning and annual returns for companies, firms and individuals.

  • 04

    Company law (ROC) filings

    Annual filings and statutory records for companies and LLPs.

  • 05

    Notices and queries

    Help responding to notices from tax authorities.

  • 06

    Compliance calendar

    A clear list of what is due, when, and who is responsible.

(03) How it works

A steady rhythm.

01

Review

We check your current registrations, filings and any gaps.

02

Plan

We set up a calendar of every due date that applies to you.

03

Prepare

Each return is prepared from your books and checked.

04

File and confirm

We file on time and share the acknowledgements with you.

(04) Who it is for

Private limited companies, LLPs and partnerships, businesses with one or more GST registrations, and owners who want compliance taken off their plate.

(05) Questions

Good to know.

Can you take over filings partway through the year?+

Yes. We review what has been filed so far, fix any gaps, and take over from the next due date.

Do you handle tax notices?+

Yes. We help you understand the notice, prepare a response and file it on the relevant portal.

Will you remind me before deadlines?+

Yes. We tell you in advance what we need from you and when, so nothing is left to the last minute.

Do you work with businesses outside Kochi?+

Yes. We work with businesses across India, online or in person.
