No more last-minute filings.

Compliance problems usually start in the books: unreconciled input credit, missed TDS deductions, unmatched vendor invoices.

Because Dair keeps your books reconciled every day, every return is prepared from clean data. Our AI matches your purchase register to GSTR-2B automatically, so you never leave input tax credit unclaimed.

Every due date sits on a shared compliance calendar, with reminders, approvals and filing receipts in one place.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which states do you cover for GST?

All Indian states and union territories, including businesses with multiple GST registrations.

Can you take over filings mid-year?

Yes. We review past filings, fix any gaps and take over from the next due date.

Do you handle tax notices?

Yes. We draft responses, file replies on the portal and represent you during assessments where needed.

Is compliance included in bookkeeping plans?

GST and TDS are included in the Growth and Scale plans. Income tax and ROC filings are available as add-ons.
