Zero to One consulting

From idea to a
launch plan.

You have a business idea but are not sure which platforms and software to use, what it will cost, or how to price what you sell. We help you work it out before you spend.

Book a free consultation
(01) Overview

Starting from zero is exciting, but the early choices are hard to undo. We help you pick the right tools, understand every cost, and set prices that cover them, so you launch with a plan rather than a guess.

(02) What is included

Everything you need to start well.

  • 01

    Idea and business model review

    We talk through your idea, your customers and how the business will make money.

  • 02

    Platforms and software

    Independent advice on what you need for selling, payments, accounting, billing and day-to-day work, and what you can skip for now.

  • 03

    Start-up and running costs

    A clear list of one-time and monthly costs: tools, subscriptions, registrations, people and fees.

  • 04

    Product and service pricing

    Prices worked out from your real costs, your margins and what similar offerings charge.

  • 05

    Registrations and compliance basics

    What to register, when, and which tax and compliance steps apply from day one.

  • 06

    Launch plan and budget

    A simple roadmap with a budget, so you know what to do first and what it will cost.

(03) How it works

A steady rhythm.

01

Listen

You tell us about your idea and what you want to achieve.

02

Research

We look at the tools, costs and prices that fit your plan.

03

Plan

You get a written plan with recommended tools, a cost sheet and prices.

04

Launch

We stay on hand as you set things up, and can continue as your accountants.

(04) Who it is for

First-time founders, people turning a side project into a business, and small teams about to launch a product or service who want clear numbers before they commit money.

(05) Questions

Good to know.

I only have an idea. Is that enough?+

Yes. That is exactly where this service starts. We help you turn the idea into a plan with real costs and prices.

Do you recommend particular software?+

We are independent. We recommend tools based on your needs and budget, not on any partnership, and we tell you when a free or simpler option will do.

What will I receive at the end?+

A written plan with recommended platforms and software, a list of start-up and monthly costs, suggested prices for your products or services, and a short launch roadmap.

Can you help after the launch?+

Yes. Many founders continue with us for bookkeeping, tax and compliance once the business is running.
