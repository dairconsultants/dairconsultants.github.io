How Dair Consultants collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Information we collect

When you use our contact or newsletter forms, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message. Our website does not use advertising cookies.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver our services and, if you subscribe, send our newsletter. We do not sell your personal data.

Client financial data

Client financial data is processed under a separate engagement agreement and data processing agreement. It is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based and logged, and it is never used to train AI models.

Service providers

We use trusted providers for hosting, email and form handling, who process data on our behalf under appropriate agreements.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or unsubscribe at any time, by emailing hello@dairconsultants.com. We handle requests in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
