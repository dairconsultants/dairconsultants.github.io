What we collect

When you contact us through this website, we collect the details you choose to share, such as your name, company, email address, phone number and message. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a proposal and, if you become a client, to deliver our services. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Information we handle for clients is covered by our engagement letter and confidentiality agreement. Access is limited to the people working on your account.

Your rights
