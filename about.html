Our story

Finance shouldn't run
a month behind.

Before Dair, our founders ran finance at fast-growing startups and at traditional accounting firms. They saw the same problem everywhere: by the time the books were ready, the decisions had already been made.

Bookkeeping was still manual, compliance was a monthly fire drill, and advice came from numbers that were weeks old. The technology to fix this existed; the firms simply weren't built around it.

So we built Dair as an AI-native finance firm. Machines handle the volume of reconciliation, categorisation and matching. Qualified accountants and consultants handle the judgement: reviewing, advising and signing off.

Today we work with more than 140 businesses, and our AI consulting arm, Dair AI Labs, helps other companies bring the same approach to their own teams.
