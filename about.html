About DAIR

Accountants who care
about the people.

DAIR Consultants is a Kochi-based finance and accounting practice. We work closely with founders and business owners, pairing careful professional work with modern tools.

How we work
(01) Why DAIR

We started DAIR to give growing businesses the kind of finance support we would want ourselves: personal, careful and clear, with modern tools doing the routine work so people can focus on advice.

We look after bookkeeping, finance consulting, tax and compliance for our clients, help businesses put AI tools to practical use, and help new founders turn an idea into a costed, priced launch plan.
